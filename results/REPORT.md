## Why the archive is non-comparable

- The 1D synthetic neural model consumed MT and gravity observations, while
  the Occam baseline consumed MT only. Their inverse observation budgets were
  unequal.
- The legacy 1D real-data producer paths did not share one versioned nRMS
  error-floor contract. Their reported values therefore cannot establish a
  cross-method ordering.
- The older 2D scores used the retired
  `section_nrms_2d_legacy_zxy_unmasked_v1` contract: one impedance component,
  edge-extrapolated periods and unequal component weighting.
- Later 2D diagnostics use
  `section_nrms_2d_profile_rotated_tetm_masked_normalized_geometry_v3`, but
  the 283–299 km field profiles are affinely mapped onto the 16 km synthetic
  model span. This changes the forward physics and is not a physical-scale
  field comparison.
- Several historical 2D tables also mix TE+TM neural warm starts with TM-only
  Gauss-Newton or stitched 1D inversions.
- The historical files predate complete immutable identities for datasets,
  checkpoints, predictions and runtimes.

## Archived 2D experiment record

The original report accumulated many development cycles: 10k/60k synthetic
training, sigma regularisation, TE+TM networks, fine-tuning, FiLM adapters,
windowed adaptation, SimPEG refinement, Occam2DMT and ModEM runs. Their raw
outputs remain under the following directories:

- `results/2d`, `results/2d-ft`, `results/60k` and `results/sreg`;
- `results/unified`, `results/hybrid2d` and `results/mpft`;
- `results/v2`, `results/v3`, `results/v4` and `results/v41`;
- `results/occam2dmt`, `results/modem2d` and `results/modem_export`.

Those artifacts preserve development history, but their numerical
cross-method tables are not reproduced here because the observation budgets,
geometry, metric versions and provenance gates were not jointly publishable.
The frozen legacy snapshot is `results/frozen_2d.json`, explicitly marked
`legacy_non_comparable`.

## Retraction of historical interpretations

The former report inferred method superiority, regional generalisation,
production readiness and state-of-the-art standing from these diagnostics.
Those interpretations are withdrawn. Within a single method and a fixed run,
some values can still help diagnose optimization behavior, but they do not
support an ordering between methods unless all current protocol gates pass.

In particular:

- a lower training-data or adaptation-profile misfit is not evidence of a
  better recovered Earth model;
- a target-profile fine-tune is not an independent field test;
- normalized-geometry nRMS is an internal consistency diagnostic, not a
  physical-scale field-resolution result;
- mixed TE/TM and gravity budgets cannot be repaired by applying the same
  scoring function after inversion;
- latency values from different implementations and hardware are descriptive
  only without a preregistered runtime protocol.

## Current comparison boundary

New comparisons must follow `docs/SOTA_PROTOCOL.md`: immutable artifacts,
held-out splits, native physical geometry, identical observation budgets,
one versioned metric/error model and uncertainty reporting. Methods that do
not satisfy every gate remain on a non-ranked diagnostic or paper-only board.

The legacy four-file renderer now requires the explicit
`--allow-legacy-diagnostic` opt-in, emits
`diagnostic_non_comparable` / `ranking_allowed=false`, validates compatible
declarations where the old files contain them, and refuses to overwrite an
existing report.
